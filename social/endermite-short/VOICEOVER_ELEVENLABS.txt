What if Minecraft's most forgotten mob finally mattered? Wildways completely improves the Endermite and adds some special items along the way.

Defeat one for a chance at an Endermite Shell. A shell and stone bricks make Endermite Bricks, with matching stairs, slabs, and walls.

Eight shells and a chest make a portable Endermite Nest. Its twelve slots keep their contents when you move it. Opening it might release another endermite though.

Leave an Eye of Ender inside. After some time, it transforms into the Eye of the Endermite.

Brew a shell into a potion of Unease. Break solid blocks, and Endermites may appear. Level 2 Unease doubles the chance.

And you'll find camps infested by endermites around the world. Watch out for hidden secrets there.

These features and lots more is part of WildWays. Releasing on November 1st.
