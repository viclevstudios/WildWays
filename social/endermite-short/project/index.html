ONE MOB.
A WHOLE
NEW TRAIL.

An Endermite used to vanish almost
as soon as you found it.
Wildways removes its fixed
despawn timer.
Defeat one, and it can drop
an Endermite Shell.
Combine one shell with stone bricks
for new bricks, stairs, slabs, and walls.
Eight shells around a chest
make an Endermite Nest:
twelve portable slots, waterlogging,
a comparator output,
and a five percent surprise
when you open it.
Leave an Eye of Ender inside.
After a random wait, it becomes the
Eye of the Endermite, one of twelve eyes for the End Portal.
Brew a shell into Unease.
Break solid blocks for a ten percent
chance to spawn a mite.
Strong Unease doubles that, and
affected creeper blasts can do it too.
Find Quarantine Grounds in the taiga,
where Endermites are already waiting.
One overlooked mob.
A whole new trail to follow.
